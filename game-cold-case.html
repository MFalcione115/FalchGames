About Cold Case

A detective roguelike where every case is procedurally generated. Work through evidence minigames to build your case, then face an interrogation boss round to close it.

Features

  • Procedurally generated cases.
  • Evidence minigames.
  • Interrogation boss rounds.

Recent updates

October 21, 2026

Ten cases make a season

A run is now a season of ten cases that ends in a finale boss. Higher Heat levels add harder rules for the whole season, and the daily case has a spoiler-free share card with streak freezes.

October 21, 2026

A cork board for big screens

On a wide display such as the unfolded Duo or an iPad, evidence goes on a cork board. Drag a clue onto a suspect to pin it, and a note hangs from a string.

October 21, 2026

Pick your edge after every case

After each case you choose one of three instincts, perks that last the rest of your run, or cash in for gold. There are 15 across three rarities, and owning three of one type switches on a bonus.

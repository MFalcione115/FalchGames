Descriptions

Short

Falch Games is the game studio of Falcione Productions, LLC, making games built around the fold of the iPhone Duo. Every game is designed for two screens and is also complete on regular iPhones.

Long

Falch Games is the game studio of Falcione Productions, LLC, a limited liability company registered in Pennsylvania. This website is owned and operated by Falcione Productions, LLC. The studio makes games for the iPhone Duo, Apple's foldable, and designs each one around the fold: a quick, one-handed experience on the cover screen, and a deeper one on the large inner display. Every game also runs well on an ordinary iPhone or iPad.

The studio builds its games from scratch, including custom audio engines and hand-made pixel and vector art. Games are free to download. Optional purchases sell time, convenience and style, never progress or exclusive power, and being away from a game never takes anything from the player.

Games in development: Hatch & Smash, Sketch Relay, Cold Case, Film Strip, Comic & Manga Reader, and VHS Duo Player. For review access, interviews or higher-resolution art, contact support@falchgames.com.

Logo

Games

Hatch & Smash

In development

Raise a kaiju folded. Wreck a city unfolded.

A pixel-art kaiju collector. Hatch monsters from eggs and build up an idle lab on the folded screen, then unfold the phone, or tap SMASH, to send a kaiju through a city and flatten as much of it as it can.

Kaiju collector and idle lab. iPhone Duo, iPhone, iPad.

Sketch Relay

Early prototype

Pass the phone. Draw it. Shout the answer.

A pass-the-phone party game in the spirit of Pictionary. One player draws a secret word against the clock while everyone else shouts guesses, then the phone moves to the next person. Propped half-folded, the Duo stands like a tiny laptop.

Party drawing game. iPhone Duo, iPhone, iPad.

Cold Case

In development

Work the evidence. Break the suspect.

A detective roguelike where every case is procedurally generated. Work through evidence minigames to build your case, then face an interrogation boss round to close it.

Detective roguelike. iPhone Duo, iPhone, iPad.

Film Strip

In development

Six moments a day. One film strip.

A daily photo app. Six random 30-minute windows open between 7 AM and 11 PM, and you take one square photo in each, with a vintage film look built in. At midnight the day develops into a 35 mm strip in your Album.

Daily photo app. iPhone Duo, iPhone, iPad.

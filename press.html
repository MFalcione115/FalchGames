Descriptions

Short

Falch Games is the game studio of Falcione Productions, LLC, making games for iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo. Every game is designed for the screens it runs on, from a single phone to the Duo's two displays.

Long

Falch Games is the game studio of Falcione Productions, LLC, a limited liability company registered in Pennsylvania. This website is owned and operated by Falcione Productions, LLC. The studio makes games for iPhone, iPad and the iPhone Duo, Apple's foldable, and designs each one for the screens it runs on: a quick, one-handed layout on iPhone, more room on iPad, and two displays working together on the Duo.

The studio builds its games from scratch, including custom audio engines and hand-made pixel and vector art. We say what each app costs and what its optional purchases include. Paid items never gate the core game, and where a game shows ads, rewarded ads are always the player's choice.

Games in development: Hatch & Smash, Cold Case, GameFold, Void Chain, and Wavefold. For review access, interviews or higher-resolution art, contact support@falchgames.com.

Logo

The mark is two screens meeting at a multicolor hinge. Keep clear space around it equal to the width of the seam, and don't stretch, recolor or add effects. Downloads: for light backgrounds (SVG) and for dark backgrounds (SVG).

Games

Hatch & Smash

In development

Raise a kaiju folded. Wreck a city unfolded.

A pixel-art kaiju collector. Hatch monsters from eggs and build up an idle lab on the folded screen, then unfold the phone, or tap SMASH, to send a kaiju through a city and flatten as much of it as it can.

Kaiju collector and idle lab. iPhone Duo, iPhone, iPad.

Cold Case

In development

Work the evidence. Break the suspect.

A detective roguelike where every case is procedurally generated. Work through evidence minigames to build your case, then face an interrogation boss round to close it.

Detective roguelike. iPhone Duo, iPhone, iPad.

Game page

GameFold

In development

Eighteen classic games for one foldable.

A collection of eighteen classic games for the iPhone Duo: six board games, six card games and six casino-style games. Play alone against the computer on the cover display, or open the phone and share the inner display with a second player. Because the casino games simulate gambling, GameFold is expected to be rated 18+.

Classic board, card and casino games. iPhone Duo.

Game page

Void Chain

In development

Clear the chain before it reaches the black hole.

A chain of planets spirals toward a black hole, and you are the last probe in orbit. Fire planets into the chain, match three or more, and clear it before the void feeds. Every run is built from the relics you find along the way.

Roguelike marble shooter. iPhone Duo, iPhone, iPad.

Wavefold

In development

A synthesizer you can jam on, made for the fold.

A synthesizer for iPhone, iPad and the iPhone Duo that is fun to jam on and deep enough for producers. It has its own audio engine, a piano roll, a MIDI looper and a set of effects.

Synthesizer and music app. iPhone Duo, iPhone, iPad.

Privacy Policy: Cold Case

Last updated November 10, 2026

This policy covers Cold Case, an app made by Falch Games, the game studio of Falcione Productions, LLC, a limited liability company registered in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Falcione Productions, LLC. Cold Case is still in development. This policy describes how it is designed to work, and we will update it before release if anything changes. The policy for this website is the site Privacy Policy.

The short version

Cold Case does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it needs no internet connection.

What the app stores on your device

To let you continue playing, the app saves the following only on your device:

  • Your game progress: rank and experience, seasons won, banked gold, tools you bought, unlocked difficulty and themes, daily case results and streak, and which tips you have seen.
  • Your settings: theme, sound, music, haptics and accessibility choices.
  • A saved run in progress, so you can leave and come back.
  • How long your recent cases took, a number shown in the app's settings and never sent anywhere.

This data stays in the app's private storage and is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you back up your device with iCloud or your computer, Apple may include this app data in that backup under Apple's own privacy terms. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What the app does not do

  • It does not connect to the internet.
  • It does not ask for or use your name, email, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, location or advertising identifier.
  • It does not use third-party code, analytics, crash reporters or advertising networks.
  • It does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Sharing your daily result

The daily case has a Share Result button. When you tap it, iOS shows its standard share sheet with a short text result (day number, grade, confidence and streak). Where that text goes is decided by you and the app you choose to share with. We never receive it.

Children

Cold Case is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask a question about this policy. Write to support@falchgames.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top of this page.

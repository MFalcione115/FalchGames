Privacy Policy

Dungeon Crawler. Effective November 25, 2026.

Who we are

Dungeon Crawler is made by Falch Games, the game studio of Falcione Productions, LLC, a limited liability company registered in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Falcione Productions, LLC. Contact us at support@falchgames.com.

The short version

The game itself collects no personal data for us. There is no account, no sign-in and no server of ours. The ads in the game are provided by Google, which collects some data to show and measure them.

What stays on your device

Your saves and settings stay on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them. If you back up your device, Apple may include them in that backup under Apple's own privacy terms.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's software in the game may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt, or another identifier limited to the app;
  • the ads you see;
  • your interactions with them, such as taps and video views;
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address;
  • crash logs and performance data.

Read how Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your tracking choice

iOS asks whether you allow the game to track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, ads still show, without the advertising identifier. You can change your answer at any time in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Players in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

You are shown Google's consent form before any ad loads. You can change your choice later from the game's Settings.

US state privacy rights

If you live in California or another US state with these rights, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for ads through the game's privacy options.

Notifications

The game can send local reminders, such as a party returning or a hero recovered. They are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.

Purchases

An ad-free subscription is planned. In-app purchases are handled by Apple, and we never see your payment details.

Children

Dungeon Crawler is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us personal information, write to us and we will delete what we hold.

Your questions and requests

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, such as emails you sent us, and ask us to correct or delete it. Write to support@falchgames.com.

Changes

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page, with a new effective date.

This policy and the website

This policy covers the game. The website has its own Privacy Policy.

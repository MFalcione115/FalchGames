Questions

Does it need Wi-Fi?

No. The game plays offline.

How do I stop personalised ads?

On your device, open Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking, and turn tracking off for the game.

How do I restore the ad-free subscription?

In the game, open Settings and tap Restore. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

My save is gone. Can I get it back?

Saves live on your device. Deleting the app deletes them, and we have no copy.

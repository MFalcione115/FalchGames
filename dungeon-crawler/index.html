Swipe Combat Pixel Roguelike

Coming to the App Store

Free, with ads. Plays offline.

Bring back the light

A retro first-person dungeon crawler with fast swipe combat. The Eclipse King has bound the moon before the sun, and the dead no longer stay buried. Six heroes, the Knight, Rogue, Assassin, Wizard, Bard and Cleric, go down into his dungeon to end it.

  • Swipe to cut, block and dodge, one foe at a time.
  • Twenty areas across five acts, with a boss at the end of each act.
  • A merchant, and forks in the road.
  • Between runs, a camp by the last fire: send heroes on expeditions for loot, meet travellers who come to trade, and train your heroes.
  • Hand-made pixel art and music.
  • On the iPhone Duo, unfold to fight in the dungeon and fold to rest at camp. Also on iPhone and iPad.

Common questions

Why didn't I get a notification when a frame opened?

Check Settings, then Notifications, in Film Strip and in iOS. Open the app at least once every few days: the app schedules about a week of reminders at a time and a nudge reminds you before they run out.

I missed a frame. Can I get it back?

Yes, with a redo. Open Today and tap Redo a frame. A redo is only spent when you keep the new photo.

How do I add friends?

Open Friends and tap the person icon to send an invite link. When someone accepts, you can share back so you see each other's strips. Friends use iCloud, so you both need to be signed in to iCloud.

How do I report or block someone?

Tap the ••• button on a friend's strip, or press and hold a comment, and choose Report or Block.

How do I get my purchases back on a new phone?

Open the Store tab and tap Restore purchases. Films come back. Unspent redos can't be restored.

How do I delete my shared photos?

Settings, then Friends and sharing, then "Stop sharing and remove my strips". To remove likes or comments linked to you, email us.

Does Film Strip work with Siri and widgets?

Yes. Add the Next Frame widget to your Home Screen or Lock Screen, and say "Hey Siri, take my Film Strip photo" or "When is my next frame in Film Strip?".

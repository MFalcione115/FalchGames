Support

For every app and game published under the Falch Games name

Falch Games is the game studio of Falcione Productions, LLC, a Pennsylvania limited liability company. If something is not working, or you have a question, comment or request, write to us and a person will answer.

Contact

Email us

Write to support@falchgames.com. Tell us which game you are playing, what you expected and what happened, and your iPhone model and iOS version if you know them. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

Support for a specific app

  • Film Strip: help with the app, its purchases and the Friends feature.
  • Games that are still in development have no app to support yet. Their pages are listed on the home page.

Purchases and refunds

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds and billing questions go through Apple's refund page. We cannot see or change your payment details.

Your data

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, write to the address above. See the privacy policy for what we collect and how long we keep it.

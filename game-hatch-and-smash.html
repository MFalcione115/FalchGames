About Hatch & Smash

A pixel-art kaiju collector. Hatch monsters from eggs and build up an idle lab on the folded screen, then unfold the phone, or tap SMASH, to send a kaiju through a city and flatten as much of it as it can. Designed for the iPhone Duo, and it also runs on iPhone and iPad.

Features

  • Free to download. Paid items never gate core progress.
  • Being away never removes coins, levels or kaiju. Your kaiju just gets lonely.
  • Kaiju come in rarities, from Grey up to Galaxy, each with its own forms.
  • Daily missions and Game Center leaderboards, with separate divisions per rarity.
  • Hand-drawn pixel art in the style of a kaiju containment terminal.

On the iPhone Duo

Folded

Hatch eggs, collect earnings and check on your kaiju in a riveted isometric lab.

Unfolded

Take a kaiju into a city for a timed smash round on the full display.

Recent updates

October 16, 2026

Daily missions

Three missions a day, one each for destroying, skill and combat, drawn from a pool of 28. Finish them for rewards, and a fresh set arrives the next day.

October 14, 2026

Ten kaiju at launch

The launch roster is ten kaiju, all of them hatching from eggs, each with its own moves and forms.

October 13, 2026

Three cities to smash

Downtown, the snowy Frostmoor and the desert Sunscar Basin. Only Downtown is open at first, and beating a city's boss opens the next.

October 10, 2026

Feeding and cleaning are gone

There's no hunger, cleaning or starving in Hatch & Smash. Your kaiju simply looks a bit sadder the longer you're away, and a single visit cheers it up. Nothing you've earned is ever taken back.

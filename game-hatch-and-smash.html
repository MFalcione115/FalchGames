About Hatch & Smash

A free pixel-art Kaiju pet, idle economy and city destruction game. On a foldable iPhone, folded is Hatch mode and unfolded is Smash mode; other iPhones and iPad switch with one key. Designed for the iPhone Duo, and it also runs on iPhone and iPad.

Frequently asked questions

What is Hatch & Smash?

A free pixel-art Kaiju pet, idle economy and city destruction game with two modes. In Hatch mode you hatch eggs, tap for coins and raise your Kaiju. In Smash mode you send it rampaging through an isometric city against the clock.

What devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad, including foldable iPhones such as the iPhone Duo. On a foldable iPhone, folded is Hatch mode and unfolded is Smash mode. Other iPhones and iPad switch with one key.

Is it free? Do I have to watch ads?

It is free to download and play, and nothing you can buy is required to progress. There are no forced ads. Rewarded videos are optional, and declining never takes anything away. In-app purchases are optional too.

What happens while I'm away?

Your contracts keep earning coins up to your vault's limit, which you can upgrade. Your Kaiju may look sad, but that never costs you coins, XP, levels or Kaiju.

How do I restore purchases or move to a new phone?

Tap RESTORE PURCHASES in the store or in the game's settings. If iCloud backup is on, the game offers to restore your progress on a new device.

How do I get help?

Email support@falchgames.com with your device, iOS version and what happened.

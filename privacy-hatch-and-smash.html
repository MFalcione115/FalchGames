Privacy Policy: Hatch & Smash

Last updated November 10, 2026

This policy covers Hatch & Smash, an app made by Falch Games, the game studio of Falcione Productions, LLC, a limited liability company registered in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Falcione Productions, LLC. Hatch & Smash is still in development. This policy describes how it is designed to work, and we will update it before release if anything changes. The policy for this website is the site Privacy Policy.

The short version

Hatch & Smash has no account system and no servers of ours, and we do not sell your data. The only third party that collects data for its own purposes is Google AdMob, which runs the optional rewarded videos.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (coins, gems, Kaiju, eggs, missions, settings and purchase records) is saved on your device. We never receive it.

iCloud backup

If you are signed in to iCloud and leave iCloud backup switched on in the game, a compact copy of your progress is stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so you can restore it on a new device. We cannot see or access it. You can turn it off in the game's settings and delete it in iOS Settings under your name, iCloud, Manage Storage.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game submits your scores and statistics to Apple's Game Center leaderboards and shows your Game Center alias and avatar in the game. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and we do not receive your name or contact details. You can sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings.

Purchases

Gems, a starter bundle, an Auto-Tapper and a season pass can be bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and we never see your card or Apple Account details. The game receives a verified record that a purchase was made so it can deliver your items. The odds of random eggs are shown before you buy.

Notifications

The game can send local notifications, for example when an egg is ready or your energy is full. They are scheduled on your device, not sent from a server, and you can turn them off in the game or in iOS Settings.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded videos, shown only when you choose to watch one. They are provided by Google AdMob. When ads are loaded or shown, Google and its partners may collect and use data such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location, device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostics, to deliver, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. This can include tracking across apps and websites owned by other companies.

Where Google requires it, the game shows Google's consent form before any ads are used, and you can change your choice in the game's ad privacy choices. On iOS you are asked whether the app may track you. If you say no, ads can still appear but are less personalised, and the game plays and rewards exactly the same. You can change this in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, Tracking.

Where the game is available

Hatch & Smash is planned for release in the United States only, and this policy is written for US law.

How long we keep data

We do not keep data about you. Data on your device stays until you delete the game. iCloud backups stay until you delete them in iOS Settings. Apple and Google keep the data they handle under their own policies.

Children

Hatch & Smash is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask a question about this policy. Write to support@falchgames.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

Film Strip is a camera app made by Falch Games, the game studio of Falcione Productions, LLC. Film Strip is a camera app that gives you six short windows a day to take a photo. It was designed so your photos stay yours. There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking in the app.

The short version

  • Your photos and film strips are stored on your device.
  • Photos leave your device only if you choose to share your strips with friends. They are then stored in your own iCloud account, and only the people you invite can open them.
  • We don't run servers that hold your photos, and we don't sell or share your data.
  • We don't track you, and we don't use advertising or analytics tools.

What stays on your device

Your daily rolls, photos, capture times, emoji captions, streaks and settings are stored on your device. Film Strip does not upload them to Falch Games.

What is stored in iCloud if you use Friends

Friends is optional. If you turn it on, Film Strip uses Apple's CloudKit, which is part of iCloud.

Falch Games can read the public CloudKit records above (likes, comments, friend requests and reports) through Apple's CloudKit dashboard. We do not have access to the contents of your private iCloud, including your photos, except photos you include in a report.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera. To take your six photos. Photos taken in the app are not added to your Photos library unless you tap Save.
  • Photos (add only). Only if you save a finished strip, recap or photo to your library. Film Strip cannot read your library.
  • Notifications. So the app can tell you when a frame opens, and about friend activity. You choose which ones in Settings.
  • iCloud. For Friends. Film Strip works without it.

Reports and moderation

You can report a friend's strip or comment. A report sends us the reason you chose, any details you add, the reported comment, and the photos from the strip being reported, so we can review it. Comments are checked on your device against a list of words that are not allowed, and people can be blocked or removed. We may ban accounts that break the rules from using Friends.

Purchases

Film stocks and redos are bought through Apple. Apple handles payment and we never see your payment details. We only learn, through StoreKit on your device, which items you own.

Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts

The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and Siri shortcuts read a small summary of today's schedule (frame times, how many you've shot, your streak) that the app saves on your device. It is not sent anywhere.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: deleting the app deletes your photos and rolls. Back up first if you want to keep them.
  • Shared strips: go to Settings, then Friends and sharing, then "Stop sharing and remove my strips".
  • Everything else: email support@falchgames.com and we will delete your likes, comments, requests and reports linked to you, usually within 30 days.

Children

Film Strip is not directed at children under 13, and the social features are meant for people 13 and older. If you believe a child has used Friends, contact us and we will remove their data.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

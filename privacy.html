Privacy Policy

Last updated October 23, 2026

This policy covers the website at falchgames.com. The website is owned and operated by Falcione Productions, LLC, a limited liability company registered in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. Falch Games is the game studio of Falcione Productions, LLC. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Falcione Productions, LLC.

The short version

  • You don't need an account to use this website, and it doesn't ask you for personal information.
  • The website doesn't use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it doesn't set cookies of its own.
  • If you email us, we use what you send only to reply to you.
  • Our games and apps each have their own privacy policy, linked below.

What happens when you visit

Like every website, ours is delivered by a hosting provider, and the provider's servers receive technical information when a page loads, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested. Our site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which is operated by GitHub, Inc. GitHub may keep server logs about visits under its own privacy statement. We do not receive or analyze those logs.

Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. That means your browser makes a request to Google's font servers, and Google receives your IP address and browser details under Google's privacy policy. We do not send Google anything else.

The website doesn't use cookies, local tracking identifiers, advertising networks, social media widgets or analytics.

If you contact us

The "Email us" button and the launch-list form on the home page open your own email app with a message addressed to support@falchgames.com. We don't receive anything until you send that email. When you do, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it to answer your message and, if you asked to hear about launches, to send a short email when a game ships or opens for testing. You can ask us to stop at any time. We don't sell your email address or share it with anyone for their own use.

Sharing

We don't sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that run the website or our email, and only as needed to do so, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep things

We keep emails we receive for as long as we need them to answer you and keep a record of the conversation. You can ask us to delete them by writing to support@falchgames.com.

Your choices and rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask us to stop emailing you. Write to support@falchgames.com and we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and you can use them by contacting us the same way.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top of this page.

What we're making

Six games in progress. Most use the fold in a different way. All of them run on regular iPhones too, except where noted.

One small studio with one big idea about phones.

A folding phone gives a game two shapes to work with: a small screen for quick moments and a large one for longer ones. We design every game for both, and make sure it still works well on an ordinary iPhone.

Falch Games is the game studio of Falcione Productions, LLC, a limited liability company registered in the state of Pennsylvania. This website is owned and operated by Falcione Productions, LLC. We make everything ourselves, from the Swift code and audio engines to the pixel art.

How we make games

Two screens, two kinds of play
Closed is for a minute in line. Open is for settling in. We don't squeeze one design into both.
Fair to play
Free to download. We sell time, convenience and style, never progress or exclusive power. Being away never takes anything from you.
Made from scratch
Our own audio engines, synthesized sound, vector and hand-drawn pixel art. Small downloads and a consistent look.
Graceful everywhere
Designed for the Duo, and complete on iPhone and iPad, with accessibility treated as part of the launch.

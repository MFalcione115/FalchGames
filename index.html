Made for the screen you have

We design each game for the device it runs on, instead of stretching one layout across all of them.

iPhone

Pick up and play. Layouts are made for one hand and for short sessions on the go.

iPad

More room. A larger display shows more of the game at once, with layouts built for the bigger screen.

iPhone Duo

Two displays. A quick layout on the cover screen, and a large two-panel canvas when you open it.

About the studio

Who is behind the games, how the company is organized and how we work.

Falch Games is the game studio of Falcione Productions, LLC, a single-member limited liability company registered in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. The company owns and operates this website and publishes its games under the Falch Games name.

Every game starts as a written specification, has its rules covered by automated tests, and goes through a release checklist before it is submitted. Optional purchases never gate progress.

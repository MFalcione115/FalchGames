Privacy Policy: GameFold

Last updated November 10, 2026

This policy covers GameFold, an app made by Falch Games, the game studio of Falcione Productions, LLC, a limited liability company registered in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Falcione Productions, LLC. GameFold is still in development. This policy describes how it is designed to work, and we will update it before release if anything changes. The policy for this website is the site Privacy Policy.

The short version

GameFold collects no data and makes no network connections. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or in-app purchases.

What stays on your device

GameFold keeps your chip balance and your appearance choice on your device, in the app's own storage. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app does not do

  • It has no accounts or sign-in.
  • It does not connect to the internet or load anything from a server.
  • It has no ads, analytics or tracking tools, and it reads no advertising identifier.
  • It has no in-app purchases and no way to pay for anything.
  • Its sound and music are generated on your device. It does not record audio.

Virtual chips

The casino-style games use virtual play-money chips only. Chips cannot be bought, have no cash value and cannot be redeemed for anything. Because these games simulate gambling, GameFold is expected to be rated 18+.

Children

GameFold is not made for children, is expected to carry an 18+ age rating, and collects no information from anyone.

Your choices and rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask a question about this policy. Write to support@falchgames.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top of this page.

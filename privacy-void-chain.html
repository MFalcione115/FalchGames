Privacy Policy: Void Chain

Last updated November 10, 2026

This policy covers Void Chain, an app made by Falch Games, the game studio of Falcione Productions, LLC, a limited liability company registered in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Falcione Productions, LLC. Void Chain is still in development. This policy describes how it is designed to work, and we will update it before release if anything changes. The policy for this website is the site Privacy Policy.

The short version

Void Chain has no account system and no servers of ours, and we do not sell your data. The ads in the game are provided by Google, which collects some data to show and measure them.

What stays on your device

Your progress (your archive, relic collection, best builds, logbook, starlight and settings) is saved on your device. A home-screen widget reads a few details, such as the daily rule, on your device only. We never receive any of it.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, the game syncs your progress between your devices using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, in your own iCloud account. We cannot see or access it. You can turn sync off in the game and delete the data in iOS Settings under your name, iCloud, Manage Storage.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game submits your scores and achievements to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, and we do not receive your name or contact details.

Advertising (Google Mobile Ads)

Void Chain is free to play and shows ads between runs, plus rewarded ads that you always choose to watch. The ads are provided by Google. When ads are loaded or shown, Google may collect data such as your device identifier, advertising data and how you interact with ads, to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. We do not link that data to you.

Where the law requires it, the game shows Google's consent form before any ads are used. As designed, the game does not ask to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

Purchases

A one-time purchase removes the between-run ads. It is made through the App Store: Apple processes the payment and we never see your card or Apple Account details.

How long we keep data

We do not keep data about you. Data on your device stays until you delete the game. iCloud data stays until you delete it. Apple and Google keep the data they handle under their own policies.

Children

Void Chain is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask a question about this policy. Write to support@falchgames.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top of this page.

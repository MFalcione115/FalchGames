Privacy Policy: Wavefold

Last updated November 10, 2026

This policy covers Wavefold, an app made by Falch Games, the game studio of Falcione Productions, LLC, a limited liability company registered in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Falcione Productions, LLC. Wavefold is still in development. This policy describes how it is designed to work, and we will update it before release if anything changes. The policy for this website is the site Privacy Policy.

The short version

Wavefold does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your projects, patterns, voice recordings, sounds, effects settings and options are saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. We cannot see them. If you delete the app, they are deleted with it, unless you exported them first.

MIDI and Bluetooth

If you connect a MIDI keyboard, controller or another app, Wavefold exchanges MIDI notes and clock signals with it on your device. If you connect a Bluetooth MIDI device, the app uses Bluetooth only for that. None of this is sent to us or anyone else.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Wavefold keeps your projects and voice recordings in your own iCloud Drive, so they are on all your devices. They are stored in your private iCloud account, under Apple's terms and privacy policy, and we cannot see or access them. You can turn this off in the app's settings, and your projects then stay on the device only.

What we do not collect

  • No account or sign-in is needed, and none exists.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools are used, and no advertising identifier is read.
  • The app does not use your camera, contacts, photos or location.
  • The microphone is used only while you record onto a voice track, and only after you allow it. The sound is saved on your device as part of your project and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can turn the permission off at any time in iOS Settings.
  • The app does not connect to any server run by us.

Files you export

When you export audio or MIDI, the file is made on your device and handed to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes next (Files, AirDrop, Messages or another app) is your choice and is governed by that app's own policy.

Wavefold Pro subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and managed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment and billing details, and we never receive your name, card or Apple Account details. The app only asks the App Store whether a subscription is active on your Apple Account.

Children

Wavefold collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and rights

You can ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or ask a question about this policy. Write to support@falchgames.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date at the top of this page.

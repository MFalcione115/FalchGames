About Falch Games

Falch Games is the game studio of Falcione Productions, LLC. We make games for iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo, and we publish every one of them under one name and one set of standards.

Our goals

What we are trying to do, in plain terms.

Games that feel at home on Apple devices

Every game is designed for the screens it runs on: a quick, one-handed layout on iPhone, more room on iPad, and two screens working together on the iPhone Duo.

Fair to the people who play

We say what each app costs and what its optional purchases include. Paid items never gate the core game, and where a game shows ads, rewarded ads are always your choice.

Made with care, from scratch

We make our own code, art and audio, so each game has a consistent look, a small download and nothing borrowed that we cannot explain.

Honest about what exists

A page says In development or Early prototype until a game is ready, and we do not show screenshots or features that are not real.

Background

Who the company is and what it makes.

Falcione Productions, LLC is a limited liability company registered in the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania. It owns and operates this website and publishes games under the Falch Games name.

The studio is currently developing five titles: Hatch & Smash, a kaiju collector; Cold Case, a detective roguelike; GameFold, a collection of eighteen classic board, card and casino games; Void Chain, a roguelike marble shooter; and Wavefold, a synthesizer. Each title's page lists the devices it supports and what stage it has reached.

How we are organized

The company is small and its structure is simple, so it is clear who is responsible for what.

  1. Falcione Productions, LLC

    The company. A single-member limited liability company registered in Pennsylvania. It owns and operates this website and the apps.

  2. Falch Games

    The game studio. This is the name players see on the App Store and on this website.

  3. Each game

    Every game has its own written specification, automated tests, release checklist, privacy policy and support page.

Because the company has a single member, one person is accountable for product decisions, support replies, privacy requests and legal compliance. Everything sent to the support address is read by that person.

How we work

The habits behind every game.

Specification first

Each game starts as a written specification: its rules, its screens for every device size, its accessibility needs and what it will and will not do.

Tested rules and layouts

Game rules and economies are covered by automated tests, and layouts are checked in Apple's simulators, including the iPhone Duo's folded and unfolded displays.

A release checklist

Before a game is submitted we work through a checklist: store listing, age rating, privacy details, purchases, and the support and legal links.

Accessibility

We design for Reduce Motion, Dynamic Type and VoiceOver, and treat accessibility as part of launching a game.

Privacy

Each app has a privacy policy that says what it collects and why. This website has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

One place for help

support@falchgames.com is the single address for every game, for purchases, privacy requests, press and partnerships.

Our intent

Why this website and these games exist.

Our intent is to build a small catalogue of well-made games that people can trust: clear about what they cost, clear about what they collect, and clear about who makes them.

This website is the public home of Falcione Productions, LLC. The Terms of Service, the Privacy Policy and Support are linked from every page and from every game.
